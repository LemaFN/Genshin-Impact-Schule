<h1>The Characters Of Genshin Impact</h1>
     <p>The Characters:</p>
<p>This is Flins</p>
<p> The Lore of Flins:<p/>
<p> <mark>Flins</mark> is the last surviving Snowland Fae, serving as the reclusive <mark>Lighthouse Keeper</mark> of Nod-Krai.
Haunted by the traumatic loss of his fallen comrades against the Abyss, he escaped reality into a long, deep slumber.
After finally awakening, his path crosses with the <mark>Traveler</mark>, to whom he surprisingly reveals his true mystical nature.<p/>

<img src="https://encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com/images?q=tbn:ANd9GcSbtPo5kf08rAThm1c8SajhF6vHJSFE6teI7Iy0hOUXTXfEsbHmVih1NYfy&s=10" alt="Genshin"width="832" height="1216">
<p>Here we got <mark>Shogun</mark> (The ruler of the Land of Eternity)</p>
<p> Her Lore:<p/>
<p> The <mark>Raiden Shogun</mark> is the vessel of the Electro Archon, seeking a frozen <mark>"Eternity"</mark> for Inazuma after losing her loved ones to tragedy.
To escape the erosion of time, she shut her soul inside a sword and created a puppet to rule the nation.
Her ruthless isolation was finally broken when the <mark>Traveler</mark> arrived and challenged her ideals. <p/>
<img src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/genshin-impact/images/e/ec/Raiden_Karte.jpg/revision/latest?cb=20211123185857&path-prefix=de" alt="Raiden" width="832" height="1216">

<p>And here would be The Traveler (you can choose between them</p>
<p> The Travelers have alot of Lore and much still hasn´t been enlightened, but here is a small cutout:<p/>
<p>
  The <mark>Traveler</mark> is a world-hopping twin who lost their sibling to a mysterious deity upon arriving in Teyvat. 
  Stripped of their original power and stuck in an unfamiliar world, they embark on a grand journey across all <mark>seven nations</mark>. 
  Along the way, they master multiple <mark>elements</mark> without a Vision while searching for answers and their lost family.
</p>
<img src="https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/5/5e/Genshin_Impact_Travelers.png?utm_source=en.wikipedia.org&utm_campaign=index&utm_content=original" alt="Travelers" width="832" height="1216">

<p> But there are some Characters that just got released Like <mark>Odette</mark>: <p/>

<p> Odette is a mysterious new figure in <mark>Teyvat</mark> whose past is still shrouded in dark secrets.
Driven by a personal vendetta, she navigates the shadows of the nations while hiding her true motivations from those around her.
Her unexpected arrival catches the attention of the Traveler, as her fate seems deeply tied to the upcoming conflicts. <p/>
<img
src="https://static.wikia.nocookie.net/gensin-impact/images/f/f3/Odette_Card.png/revision/latest?cb=20260703040217" alt="Odette">
<p> And we got Varesa<p/>
<p>
  <mark>Varesa</mark> is an easy-going warrior and orchard owner from Natlan's <mark>Collective of Plenty</mark>. 
  Adored by local children as the "Masked Hero", she possesses immense martial strength alongside a legendary, <mark>insatiable appetite</mark> for food. 
  Despite her relaxed lifestyle, she is highly dedicated to defending her nation against any incoming threats.
</p>
