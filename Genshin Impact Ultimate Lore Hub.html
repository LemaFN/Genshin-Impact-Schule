Teyvat Lore & Character Compendium

The Characters Of Genshin Impact

A comprehensive journey across Teyvat, featuring legendary heroes, enigmatic travelers, and the supreme Fatui Harbingers.

Featured Characters

Nod-Krai

Flins

Lighthouse Keeper of Nod-Krai
The Lore of Flins

Flins is the last surviving Snowland Fae, serving as the reclusive Lighthouse Keeper of Nod-Krai. Haunted by the traumatic loss of his fallen comrades against the Abyss, he escaped reality into a long, deep slumber. After finally awakening, his path crosses with the Traveler, to whom he surprisingly reveals his true mystical nature.

Inazuma

Shogun

Ruler of the Land of Eternity
Her Lore

Here we got Shogun (The ruler of the Land of Eternity). The Raiden Shogun is the vessel of the Electro Archon, seeking a frozen "Eternity" for Inazuma after losing her loved ones to tragedy. To escape the erosion of time, she shut her soul inside a sword and created a puppet to rule the nation. Her ruthless isolation was finally broken when the Traveler arrived and challenged her ideals.

Outlander

The Traveler

World-Hopping Twin (Aether / Lumine)
Their Journey

And here would be The Traveler (you can choose between them). The Traveler is a world-hopping twin who lost their sibling to a mysterious deity upon arriving in Teyvat. Stripped of their original power and stuck in an unfamiliar world, they embark on a grand journey across all seven nations. Along the way, they master multiple elements without a Vision while searching for answers and their lost family.

Fontaine / Teyvat

Odette

Mysterious New Figure
Her Lore

But there are some Characters that just got released Like Odette: Odette is a mysterious new figure in Teyvat whose past is still shrouded in dark secrets. Driven by a personal vendetta, she navigates the shadows of the nations while hiding her true motivations from those around her. Her unexpected arrival catches the attention of the Traveler, as her fate seems deeply tied to the upcoming conflicts.

Natlan

Varesa

Masked Hero of Natlan
Her Lore

Varesa is an easy-going warrior and orchard owner from Natlan's Collective of Plenty. Adored by local children as the "Masked Hero", she possesses immense martial strength alongside a legendary, insatiable appetite for food. Despite her relaxed lifestyle, she is highly dedicated to defending her nation against any incoming threats.
